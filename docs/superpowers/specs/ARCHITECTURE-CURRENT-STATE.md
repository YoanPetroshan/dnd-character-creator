# Architecture: current state (split)

This document was split in slice S15 (audit MAINT-03) into `docs/architecture/`. Code comments and older notes that say
"see ARCHITECTURE-CURRENT-STATE.md (W7 section)" mean the section of that name in one of these files:

- `docs/architecture/overview.md`: What the app is, the data flow, how to orient yourself.
- `docs/architecture/engine.md`: The ASI-or-feat mechanic and the backend layout.
- `docs/architecture/data.md`: Where the rules data comes from and the sourcebook phases (4a to 4c-ii).
- `docs/architecture/i18n.md`: The bilingual layer (BG / EN): the overview and the recipes.
- `docs/architecture/offline-pwa.md`: Service extraction, the offline build, the worker, the lazy PDF stack, the PWA and the Pages deploy (O0 to O5).
- `docs/architecture/testing.md`: Golden baselines and rebaseline mode, the frozen English message snapshot, the fuzz harness, the character-test harness, xdist, the clean-clone smoke, test-suite health.
- `docs/architecture/known-gaps.md`: What a reader should not mistake for a bug, and the design questions with their status (answered ones keep their recorded answer).
- `docs/architecture/CHANGELOG-slices.md`: frozen slice history (every dated slice section).
