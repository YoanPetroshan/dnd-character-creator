## SLICE 10

Design: `draft.ruleset` ∈ "2014"|"2024"|"mixed"; missing/invalid → "mixed" (invalid also warning `ruleset-invalid`). ASI policy in one function (`_asi_policy(ruleset, race, subrace, bg) -> (species_on, background_on, warnings)`):
- "2014": species ASIs apply; background ASIs ignored and its `:ability-bonus` template not emitted; if bg had them warning `ruleset-2014-background-asi-ignored`.
- "2024": species ASIs ignored (fixed and choice; species ability template not emitted), warning `ruleset-2024-species-asi-ignored` if species had any; if bg has no `ability_bonus_options` warning `ruleset-2024-background-no-asi`; if bg grants no feat warning `ruleset-2024-background-no-origin-feat`.
- "mixed": if bg has `ability_bonus_options` AND species has any ASI → suppress species ASI + warning `asi-double-dip-prevented`; else both apply.
All ruleset conflicts are WARNINGS (export never blocked by a legal house-rule mix); hard problems unchanged (`unknown-species`/`unknown-background`/`unknown-subspecies` for sources-filtered records). A selection left for a suppressed ability template must appear in `pruned` (verify by test).
Catalog tagging `"ruleset": "2024"|"2014"`: races/backgrounds from `edition == "one"`; feats from `RULESET_2024_SOURCES = {"XPHB","XDMG","XMM","FRHoF","EFA","ABH","LFL"}` in reference_data.py (RHW feats = 2014); subraces inherit parent race; SRD "2014". `ruleset_allows(record, kind, ruleset)`: "2014" hides 2024 species/backgrounds/feats; "2024" hides 2014 backgrounds only; "mixed" hides nothing. Visibility only hides records in `/api/catalog` and ASI feat options; `resolve()` never rejects on ruleset alone. Old drafts (no ruleset) resolve as "mixed"; `emptyDraft.ruleset = "mixed"`, `loadInitial` fills it.
